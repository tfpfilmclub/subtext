A NOTE FROM THE EDITOR

A beginning.

Subha Nawer Aroni

Editor-in-chief

Warm wishes to all our readers.

This issue marks a beginning of our very first step into what we hope will become a long and meaningful journey. With this debut edition, we wanted to create a space shaped by the voices, ideas, and creative spirit of the students of TFP.

We received an overwhelming number of writings from TFP students for this issue. While we couldn’t include every piece in this edition, we are truly grateful to everyone who submitted their work. The amount of passion and talent we witnessed through these submissions made this first issue feel even more special, and we look forward to featuring many of them in our upcoming segments.

We are also excited to present an interview with Tanim Noor, the director of Utshob, along with a piece by S.K. Shuvo Sadique on cinema and his first Mro-language film, Dear Mother.

Thank you for being here at the very start. Stay with us—the best is yet to come.

Subha Nawer Aroni

সভাপতির শুভেচ্ছাবার্তা

তাকিয়া মোনাও-ওয়ারা কথা

ম্যাগাজিন প্রকাশ করা আমাদের প্যানেলের প্রাথমিক পরিকল্পনার মধ্যে ছিল না। ক্লাবের মডারেটর সৈয়দ শাহজাদা আল কারীম স্যার প্রথম দিনেই প্রস্তাবটা দেন—উনি চেয়েছিলেন একটি ম্যাগাজিন হোক। আমাদের এই ছোট বিভাগে, যেখানে শিক্ষার্থীর সংখ্যা তুলনামূলক কম, আর চলচ্চিত্রের পাশাপাশি আরও দুটি বিষয় অন্তর্ভুক্ত—সেখানে শিক্ষার্থীদের লেখা সংগ্রহ করে একটি সম্পূর্ণ ‘চলচ্চিত্র বিষয়ক’ ম্যাগাজিন প্রকাশ করা রীতিমতো চ্যালেঞ্জের ব্যাপার ছিল।

তবু ভীষণ গর্বের সঙ্গে বলতে চাই, ক্লাবের সহ-সভাপতি সুবহা নাওয়ার অরনী পুরো বিষয়টি দারুণভাবে সামলে নিয়েছে। দীর্ঘ সময় ধরে শিক্ষার্থীদের লেখা সংগ্রহ করা, সেগুলো বাছাই ও গুছিয়ে নেওয়া—সবশেষে সীমিত সামর্থ্যের মধ্যেই ম্যাগাজিনটি প্রকাশযোগ্য করে তোলাও সম্ভব হয়েছে।

And finally, SUBTEXT comes to life!

প্রিয় টিএফপি ও অন্যান্য বিশিষ্টজন, টিএফপি ফিল্ম ক্লাবের পক্ষ থেকে আপনাদের জন্য আমাদের এই ছোট উপহার—SUBTEXT.

FROM THE FOUNDING CHAIRMAN / ISSUE 01

A guiding light.

Dr. Abu Jafar Md. Shafiul Alam Bhuiyan

Professor, Department of Television, Film and Photography

I express my sincere appreciation for the inaugural issue of the TFP Film Club magazine, Subtext, published on the occasion of the second TFP Creative Excellence Award ceremony. Launching a film magazine to mark the Creative Excellence Award is a brilliant, innovative, and thoughtful idea. This inaugural issue contains the views and opinions of future filmmakers and film scholars on film art. This is a portrayal of the brilliance, passion, and commitment of young minds pursuing film and television studies. The stories told in this issue are unmatched by any other publication, as they depict raw and genuine emotions. This publication saw the light because of the film club leadership’s diligence, hard work, and commitment to innovation.

I would like to believe that this endeavor will continue in the future. A film magazine has the potential to be a guiding light for filmmakers and the national film industry, carrying the thoughts and views of junior and senior film scholars. It can be a platform for debate on the nature of national cinema and the importance of cinema as an art form. It can serve as a platform for testing novel filmmaking ideas. After almost 100 years of filmmaking, Bangladesh has yet to develop its own film style. Thought-provoking articles from different perspectives can help create a distinct style for Bangladeshi cinema.

This magazine should regularly produce thematic issues on Bangladeshi films, films from other countries, and renowned filmmakers. However, publishing regular issues is an arduous task, requiring continuous support and encouragement from the department, financial support from sponsors, and creative labor from a dedicated army of creative and innovative youth. However, to minimize publication costs, it can only be published online. Regardless of the magazine’s form, efforts should be made to engage students and faculty members from other departments as writers to cultivate a broader readership.

At the end of the day, it is a rewarding experience for students as it offers an opportunity to learn and apply decision-making and creative skills. The department and faculty should lend their support for its success. I would be happy to assist you in any way possible.

Good luck to Subtext!

FROM THE CHAIRPERSON / ISSUE 01

Beyond the obvious.

Saiyeed Shahjada Al Kareem

Chairperson, Department of Television, Film and Photography
University of Dhaka · Moderator, TFP Film Club

The launch of SUBTEXT marks more than the publication of a magazine. It signals an intellectual coming-of-age for the TFP Film Club. A first issue is always an act of courage. It declares intent, taste, and an ethical position toward cinema and criticism. In choosing SUBTEXT as its title, the editorial team commits itself to looking beyond the obvious, to reading cinema not merely as image and story, but as ideology, affect, history, and resistance.

As Chair of the Department of Television, Film and Photography, University of Dhaka, I see this initiative as a natural extension of what we strive to cultivate within our learning environment: critical attention, formal rigor, and the confidence to ask difficult questions. Cinema today circulates faster than thought; SUBTEXT proposes a necessary pause. It invites students to slow down, to analyze, to argue, and to write cinema seriously, without losing the pleasure that first drew us to the moving image.

As Moderator of the TFP Film Club, I am particularly proud of the collective labor behind this issue. A film club is not merely a screening space; it is a discursive commons. SUBTEXT transforms that commons into print, into a record of debates, disagreements, obsessions, and emerging voices. This magazine should not aim for consensus or politeness. Its strength will lie in its willingness to probe form, politics, language, and power.

I congratulate the editorial team for taking this decisive first step. May SUBTEXT remain restless, self-critical, and intellectually bold, and may future issues continue to unsettle easy readings while nurturing a rigorous culture of film thought within and beyond our department.
