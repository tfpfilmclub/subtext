## Images

Variants are in `public/assets/enhanced/`, mapped in `public/image-enhancements.js`. They are labelled AI-upscaled on article pages with an original comparison link. AI upscaling can estimate detail; it is not newly recovered source detail. Original WebPs and all extracted originals remain unchanged.

Prompt set: identity-preserving, resolution-only archival upscale, preserve source aspect ratio/framing/composition/people/clothing/panel boundaries/colors; conservative deblocking and modest sharpening; keep unresolved details soft; no invented features, new objects, text, cropping, beautification or colorization. Each of the four source images was supplied as an edit target, and the results were visually reviewed before selection. The resulting PNGs were converted to WebP for web delivery. Sharing JPEGs were regenerated from the selected web artwork.
